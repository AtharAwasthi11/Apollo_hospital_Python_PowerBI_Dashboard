## 📌 Executive Summary
Healthcare organizations face challenges in **patient management, operational efficiency, and financial sustainability**.  
This project builds a **data pipeline (Python)** and an **interactive dashboard (Power BI)** to provide Apollo Hospital with a **360° view of operations**.  

The solution empowers administrators to:
- Monitor **real-time patient inflow and demographics**.  
- Track **bed occupancy, consultation times, and wait periods**.  
- Evaluate **financial KPIs** across departments and payers.  
- Assess **clinical outcomes** and patient satisfaction.  

By integrating **ETL automation, advanced DAX measures, and drill‑down dashboards**, the project transforms raw hospital data into **actionable insights**.

---

## 🚀 Key Features
### 👩‍⚕️ Patient Demographics
- Age & gender distribution across departments.  
- Trends in patient inflow segmented by specialty.  

### ⚡ Operational Efficiency
- Average wait times per department.  
- Bed occupancy rates with predictive alerts.  
- Consultation duration analysis for resource optimization.  

### 💰 Financial KPIs
- Revenue breakdown by department.  
- Payer mix analysis (insurance vs. self‑pay).  
- Billing cycle efficiency and overdue accounts.  

### 🧪 Clinical Outcomes
- Treatment success rates by specialty.  
- Patient feedback sentiment analysis.  
- Correlation between resource allocation and outcomes.  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn)** → ETL, cleaning, and exploratory analysis.  
- **Power BI** → Interactive dashboards, KPI cards, maps, drill‑downs, and DAX measures.  
- **Power Query (M Language)** → Data modeling and transformations.  
- **Visualization Layer** → KPI cards, bar charts, maps, doughnut charts, and trend lines.
